# Advanced-AI

<h1>Auto Vision: Advanced AI for Damage Detection and Categorization</h1>

<h2>Project Overview</h2>

<p>Auto Vision is an advanced AI group project focused on the automated detection and categorization of vehicle damage. The system is designed to identify and classify damage types, such as scratches and dents, and assess their severity (minor, moderate, or severe). This technology has the potential to streamline insurance claims processes, improve vehicle inspection efficiency, and enhance the accuracy of damage assessments.</p>

<h2>Project Goals and Objectives</h2>

<p>The primary goals of this project are:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Accurate Damage Detection:</strong> To develop a system capable of reliably detecting various types of vehicle damage, including scratches and dents.</li>
    <li><strong>Severity Categorization:</strong> To accurately classify the severity of detected damage into predefined categories (minor, moderate, and severe).</li>
    <li><strong>Automation:</strong> To automate the damage assessment process, reducing the need for manual inspection and improving efficiency.</li>
    <li><strong>Advanced AI:</strong> Utilize state-of-the-art AI techniques to achieve high levels of accuracy and robustness.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Features</h2>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Damage Detection:</strong> Identifies scratches, dents, and other forms of vehicle damage in images or video.</li>
    <li><strong>Severity Classification:</strong> Categorizes detected damage as minor, moderate, or severe.</li>
    <li><strong>Image Processing:</strong> Utilizes computer vision techniques for image analysis and processing.</li>
    <li><strong>Machine Learning:</strong> Employs machine learning models for damage detection and classification.</li>
    <li><strong>Scalability:</strong> Designed to be scalable and adaptable to different use cases and datasets.</li>
    <li><strong>Potential Applications:</strong> The system can be used in a variety of applications, including:
        <ul>
            <li>Insurance claims processing</li>
            <li>Vehicle inspection</li>
            <li>Automotive repair estimation</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
</ul>
